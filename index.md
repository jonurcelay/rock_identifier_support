### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why wasn't my rock identified correctly?**  
A: Try taking the photo with better lighting and a clearer view of the rock. Low confidence usually means the image is blurry or dark.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**  
A: Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.

**Q: Is the app accurate?**  
A: Our AI is highly accurate for common rocks and minerals, but results may vary with image quality.
